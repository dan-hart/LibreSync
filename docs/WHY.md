# Why LibreSync

LibreSync exists to make **local-first** data exchange simple, private, and reliable without a cloud dependency.

## Principles
- **Local-only**: devices connect directly on the LAN or a private overlay.
- **E2EE by default**: the engine encrypts payloads and local state automatically.
- **Consent-driven**: explicit device pairing and revocation.
- **Open and free**: AGPLv3 ensures the core stays open.

## What makes it different
- No accounts, no hosted relay, no third-party storage.
- A stable, minimal API for embedding in apps.
- Logical record sync for structured state, plus file adapters for arbitrary data.
- Always-on desktop device (LibreSyncAlwaysOn) can keep data refreshed on your LAN.

## Who it’s for
- App developers who want sync without centralized infrastructure.
- Users who care about privacy and local control.
- Teams who need simple, local data exchange.
